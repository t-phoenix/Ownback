How it works

Four steps, all yours.

01Add a purchase

Snap the receipt, or let Ownback find the invoice in your Gmail.

02Save a share

2–10% of it (5% by default) goes into USDC jars, split between the maker, the companies behind it and the raw materials.

03Approve the buy

When a jar is ready, you see the live price and every fee. Nothing buys until you tap.

04Own it

Tokenized shares land in your own wallet, with a public proof of every step.

Is this real money?

Yes. The app runs on mainnet. You hold USDC, and the shares you buy are tokenized and go to your own wallet.

Does Ownback buy anything on its own?

No. Every buy needs your approval. You see the live price, the quantity and the fees in dollars before you sign.

Do I need crypto to start?

You sign in with Google. You only ever hold USDC; network fees are shown in dollars and capped by your own limit.

Why is there a waitlist?

Because it's real money, we're opening slowly and adding people a few at a time.
