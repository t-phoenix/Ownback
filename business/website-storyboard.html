Launch app, and who gets in

Launch app button

Always in the top bar. Someone not on the list gets the waitlist sheet. Someone approved goes on to app.ownback.tech.

The real lock

Anyone can type the app's address, so the app checks the signed-in Google email against the same allowlist and shows "You're on the list" to everyone else.

Below the film

A short FAQ (Is this real money? Which countries? What does it cost?), then a footer with X, LinkedIn, privacy and terms.

Sound

Off by default. A speaker toggle adds a soft score, the phone buzz, box thuds and coin clinks.

Responsive plan

Same film, three budgets

DesktopOne full WebGL world, pinned scenes, cursor parallax and soft shadows.
TabletSame world with fewer particles. Captions sit over the lower third.
PhoneLower resolution with tilt in place of the cursor, and captions stacked under the scene. With reduced motion or on old phones, each scene becomes a still frame with a fade.
Hidden admin · ownback.tech/admin (not linked anywhere, not indexed)

How I'd build it

Build plan

  • Where: a new site/ Next.js app in this repo with its own Vercel project, serving ownback.tech. The app moves to app.ownback.tech.
  • 3D: the scenes on this page are the creatives. On the site they become one continuous Three.js world driven by GSAP ScrollTrigger, with soft shadows, bloom on the gold and a camera path that links each scene to the next.
  • Waitlist data: one waitlist table in your existing Neon Postgres. Emails stay private and never appear in logs.
  • Admin login: a password you set in Vercel, with a signed cookie. Rate-limited.
  • Abuse: a hidden honeypot field and a per-IP rate limit on the form. No CAPTCHA.
